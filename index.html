One question. Three dimensions.

Study highlights

How does susceptibility to neurotoxic insults change as the brain develops?

The study evaluates reactive oxygen species, lipid peroxidation and MTT reduction across postnatal stages following excitotoxic, mitochondrial and oxidative insults.

Integrated interpretation

The susceptibility of the developing brain depends on both postnatal age and the neurotoxic mechanism examined.
